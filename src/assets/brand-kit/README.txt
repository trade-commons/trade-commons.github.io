Global Trade Commons — brand masters

Three files, and nothing derived from them. Everything the site actually loads is generated by
scripts/build-brand.py and written to src/assets/brand/ and public/. Change a master, re-run that
script, commit what it writes.

  icon-transparent.png            the network mark
  logo-horizontal-transparent.png mark, wordmark and tagline, set horizontally
  wordmark-transparent.png        wordmark alone

These are raster artwork cropped from the original concept image, not vector. Two consequences
matter when using them anywhere else.

The alpha channel they ship with is wrong. The artwork was composited on white and the background
then removed, which left most of the canvas at alpha 1 to 40 instead of 0 and a white halo around
every stroke. On a white page that is invisible; on anything darker it reads as a milky rectangle.
build-brand.py flattens each master back onto white and knocks the white out properly, so use its
output rather than these files directly.

The mark does not survive 16px. It is five nodes over five overlapping arcs, and below about 24px
those strokes fall under one pixel. build-brand.py thickens them for the favicon, which makes it
legible rather than good. A small-size variant with fewer elements is the real fix, and it is
vector work on the original artwork.

The earlier kit also carried icon-16 through icon-512, a favicon.ico, logo-horizontal-white and
tagline-lockup. All were dropped: the icon sizes and the .ico are regenerated better by the script,
tagline-lockup was byte-identical to logo-horizontal-transparent on a 5px taller canvas, and
logo-horizontal-white was the same artwork flattened onto white.
