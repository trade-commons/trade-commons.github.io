The component catalogue is organised by what a thing is. This page is the same material organised by
the job somebody has, because an organisation arriving here knows its own role long before it knows
which component it needs.

Two things are worth reading off it. **What a role runs**, which is the shortest answer to "where do
we start", and **who that serves**, which is the part people underestimate. Almost nothing here is
deployed for its operator's own benefit alone. A registration authority issues identity anchors so
that somebody else can check an identifier. A producer publishes a passport so that a buyer, a
customs administration or a regulator can read it without asking. The value of running any of this
accrues mostly to the party on the other side, which is why adoption stalls without enough of the
chain acting at once.

Most entries below are proposed and do not exist yet. The roles are real regardless, and they are
what the components are being built against.
